Latin Modern Roman
==================

This directory includes an unmodified copy of lmroman10-regular.otf from
the Latin Modern font family, version 2.004, by Boguslaw Jackowski and
Janusz M. Nowacki (on behalf of TeX user groups).

Latin Modern is based on Donald E. Knuth's Computer Modern fonts. The font
is distributed under the GUST Font License; see GUST-FONT-LICENSE.txt in
this directory. The copy used here comes from Debian's fonts-lmodern
package.
